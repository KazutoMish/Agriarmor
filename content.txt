CHAPTER I 
INTRODUCTION 
 
Business Description 
 
In the Philippines, farmers, particularly in areas like the Cordillera 
Administrative Region and Nueva Vizcaya, face a recurring issue of having to 
discard tons of fresh tomatoes due to an oversupply. This problem is exacerbated 
by crashing farmgate prices and a severe lack of cold storage facilities. 
Concurrently, the Philippines experiences high humidity, frequent rainfall, and salty 
coastal air, which accelerate the oxidation and rusting of iron and steel 
infrastructure, vehicles, and industrial equipment. AGRIARMOR addresses a 
significant agricultural crisis by transforming discarded, surplus tomatoes into a 
non-toxic, eco-friendly rust remover specifically designed for the tropical climate of 
the Philippines. 
Traditional maintenance methods for rust involve physical scraping or wire 
brushing, applying chemical rust converters or inhibitors, and sealing surfaces with 
protective topcoats or galvanization. However, AGRIARMOR offers a solution by 
utilizing the natural organic acids found in surplus tomatoes. Its products effectively 
dissolve iron oxides, providing safer, non-toxic alternatives to harsh mineral acid 
converters for industries such as automotive, maritime, and construction. 
Additionally, this innovative approach delivers vital economic relief directly to local 
farming cooperatives.

Company Name and Background 
 
AGRIARMOR is a company focused on transforming regional agricultural 
crises into valuable industrial solutions by repurposing discarded crop waste into 
non-toxic metal protection products. AgriArmor derives its name from the fusion of 
"Agri," representing its agricultural roots in upcycling surplus tomato crops from 
local farmers, and "Armor," signifying the strong, durable rust protection its bio-
based solutions provide to metal infrastructure. The firm was established to 
address two significant issues: the substantial financial losses faced by farmers in 
Northern Luzon, who often have to dispose of large quantities of surplus tomatoes 
in areas like CAR and Nueva Vizcaya, and the rapid corrosion of steel 
infrastructure caused by high humidity and coastal salt spray. 
Through combining sustainable farming practices with eco-friendly 
manufacturing, AGRIARMOR’s RedRevive is a product that utilizes the natural bio-
active organic acids found in surplus tomato pulp to safely dissolve iron oxides 
without the use of harsh mineral acids. RedRevive offers a safer surface 
preparation solution for the maritime, automotive, and construction industries, prior 
to processes like wire brushing and topcoat sealing. Ultimately, the company 
provides essential economic relief directly to rural farming cooperatives while 
promoting environmental sustainability.

Design Interpretation and Psychology of Color 
 
The AGRIARMOR’s Logo shows a shield split in half to represent the 
company’s dual mission. The left side shows a pulp to represent and highlight 
agricultural roots, while the right side shows a metallic shield with baybayin 
characters of AGRIARMOR symbolizing steel protection. The bold emerald green 
border is associated with sustainability, eco-friendly green chemistry, and crop 
renewal the emblem incorporates a grounded black font for "AGRI," conveying 
corporate authority. The word "ARMOR" is rendered in a polished silver chrome 
typeface to evoke strength and durability. The design is highlighted with pure white 
accents, signifying clean, non-toxic chemistry. Completing the visual composition 
is the tagline which perfectly balances agricultural waste recovery with heavy-duty 
corrosion. 
 
Tagline 
“Saving crops, shielding steels” 
 
The tagline of AGRIARMOR bridges sustainable agricultural sourcing with 
steel protection. “Saving crops” highlights our circular-economy model of upcycling 
surplus agricultural yield to minimize waste, while “Shielding steel” means a 
promise of metal protection without harsh chemicals. Together, the tagline 
captures both our eco-friendly principle and commercial utility in a memorable 
value proposition.

Vision 
To become the leading eco-friendly industrial solution in Southeast Asia by 
transforming agricultural waste into high-performance corrosion defense that 
protects infrastructure while empowering local farming communities. 
Mission 
Safeguard metal infrastructure throughout the Philippines from tropical 
corrosion through the creation of effective, non-toxic rust removers using surplus 
tomatoes that provide sustainable economic relief to local farmers, reduce 
agricultural waste, and replace harsh chemical treatments with environmentally 
friendly technology.

- 
To become the leading eco-friendly alternative to toxic chemical rust 
removers in the Philippines.

Nature of Business 
 
AGRIARMOR operates as a sustainable manufacturing company that 
converts agricultural waste into eco-friendly metal maintenance products. The 
business operates using a dual-impact model. It purchases surplus, unharvested, 
or discarded tomatoes directly from farming cooperatives in regions such as the 
Cordillera Administrative Region and Nueva Vizcaya. The company extracts 
natural organic acids, including citric and oxalic acids, from these tomatoes to 
produce bio-based rust removers and corrosion inhibitors. By offering these non-
toxic, chemical-free solutions—led by its product, RedRevive—to industries like 
maritime, automotive, construction, heavy equipment, or simple home rust 
problem, AGRIARMOR serves as both an agricultural waste management partner 
and a provider of high-performance industrial maintenance solutions.

Environmental Trends and Future Outlook 
 
Political 
The enterprise operates within national policies that actively promote 
sustainable agriculture and green manufacturing initiatives. Government 
support for agricultural waste management and circular economy models 
creates opportunities for institutional recognition and potential micro-grant 
funding for eco-friendly ventures. However, small-scale chemical 
manufacturing remains subject to local government business permits, 
sanitation clearances, and strict compliance with national consumer safety 
standards for household chemicals. 
 
Economic 
Economic Factors Fluctuations in local tomato supply and seasonal 
market pricing directly influence raw material sourcing costs. Utilizing 
market-reject and overripe crops serves as a strategic cost hedge, 
maintaining low Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) even during peak harvest price 
spikes. Furthermore, ongoing inflationary pressures make cost-effective 
bio-based alternatives highly attractive to price-sensitive retail consumers

Product Description 
AGRIARMOR’s RedRevive is a highly effective organic rust remover 
crafted from surplus tomatoes cultivated in the Cordillera Administrative 
Region and Nueva Vizcaya. By utilizing these excess tomatoes, which 
would otherwise go to waste, RedRevive provides an environmentally 
friendly solution for rust removal. This innovative product not only helps to 
protect and extend the life of steel surfaces but also supports local farmers 
by creating a market for their surplus crops. In doing so, it contributes to 
sustainable agriculture while ensuring that both the environment and the 
livelihoods of farmers are safeguarded.  
 
Features 
RedRevive contains a bio-based active matrix made from 
surplus tomatoes. It is a biodegradable, non-corrosive, and non-toxic 
formulation. Its thick gel consistency ensures high surface adhesion 
and replaces harmful, petroleum-derived solvent chemicals with a 
safe, food-grade agricultural derivative. This not only eliminates 

--- PAGE 39 ---
 
Page | 37  
 
School of Business Administration and Accountancy 
hazardous waste disposal fees but also promotes a safer workplace 
environment. 
Benefits 
Tomato acts as an organic acid donor & chelating agent 
contains natural citric, malic, and oxalic acids that dissolves iron 
oxides while natural chelating compounds bind free ferric/ferrous 
ions to prevent re-deposition on metal surfaces. 
Xanthan Gum transforms a thin liquid wash into a high-
viscosity, clingy gel matrix, this prevents fluid runoff on vertical, 
overhead, or complex machinery surfaces, keeping active acids in 
continuous contact with rusted areas. 
Mild Surfactant acts as a wetting agent & penetration 
enhancer. It Reduces surface tension of the liquid, allowing the acid 
formulation to penetrate deep into microscopic pores, cracks, and 
pitted rust. It also emulsifies grease, dirt, and oils coating the metal 
so active organic acids can directly access the oxidized surface. 
Citric Acid binds to the iron ions to form a water-soluble iron-
citrate complex that easily rinses away, offering a safe, bio-based 
alternative to harsh mineral acids without damaging the underlying 
metal. 
Sodium Benzoate forms a thin, protective molecular barrier 
over bare, freshly cleaned steel to block oxygen and moisture.

Additionally, its antimicrobial properties stabilize organic plant 
extracts in liquid formulations, preventing microbial growth and 
extending product shelf life. 
Baking soda acts as a natural buffer that neutralizes foul, sour 
odors caused by organic fermentation without degrading the active 
acid profile.  
AGRIARMOR’s product restores steel and iron equipment 
without causing base-metal erosion. It reduces exposure to 
workplace hazards and lowers PPE compliance costs. This product 
extends the capital lifespan of machinery while reducing overall 
operational and maintenance (O&M) risk profiles for industrial 
clients. 
 
Segmentation/Varieties

consumer retail, commercial agriculture, and heavy industrial plant

• RedRevive Pro: 1L cans for agricultural machinery.

AGRIARMOR’s RedRevive showcases a high degree of innovation,

excess crop production and market devaluation while converting waste into

products. This not only provides farmers with new revenue but also supports

environmentally responsible practices in agriculture.

Quality Control 
Incoming Quality Control (IQC) 
Quality assurance begins at the raw material procurement stage. 
Incoming batches of raw tomatoes undergo rigorous visual and manual 
sorting to ensure they are free from mold, rot, and foreign debris, 
maintaining a maximum tolerance of 5% physical bruising. Any batch failing 
to meet these standards is rejected and returned to the market vendor. 

--- PAGE 68 ---
 
Page | 66  
 
School of Business Administration and Accountancy 
Concurrently, chemical additives such as acid boosters and thickeners are 
inspected against Certificates of Analysis and visual checks to verify they 
are dry, unclumpy powders of technical or food grade, with any unsealed or 
degraded materials returned immediately. 
In-Process Quality Control (IPQC) 
During the blending and processing phase, technical staff 
continuously monitor critical chemical and physical parameters. The acidity 
of the mixture is measured using a calibrated digital pH meter to ensure it 
strictly remains within the optimal range of pH 2.0 to 2.5. If the pH exceeds 
2.5, additional citric acid is incorporated, whereas a pH below 2.0 is 
adjusted with distilled water. In addition, the viscosity and consistency of the 
gel are evaluated using a standard slump or flow test to ensure it forms a 
medium-thick gel capable of clinging to vertical rusted surfaces; if the 
formulation remains too runny, xanthan gum is added in measured 
increments. 
Final Quality Control (FQC) 
Prior to distribution, finished product batches and packaging undergo 
rigorous performance and integrity testing. Every batch undergoes a 
standard metal coupon test, where a sample rusted nail or plate must 
demonstrate complete surface rust breakdown within 30 minutes; non-

Risk Management 
 
AGRIARMOR maintains proactive risk management to ensure safety during 
product operation. To protect supply stability, and product performance for 
RedRevive. To prevent microbial spoilage caused by natural tomato sugars, 
sodium benzoate is added to stabilize the formula, extend shelf life, and act as a 
passivating agent that blocks post-treatment flash rust. 
 
Evaluation of Weaknesses 
Raw Material Volatility: Heavy dependence on seasonal tomato crops 
leaves supply vulnerable to typhoons and harvest gaps. 
Storage & Spoilage: Raw fruit takes up significant space and spoils 
quickly. Operations enforce a 24-hour turnaround to process incoming 
tomatoes immediately into stabilized, shelf-stable liquid concentrates. 
Buyer Skepticism: B2B industrial clients often assume eco-friendly 
chemicals are weaker than harsh mineral acids. AGRIARMOR overcomes 
this through free sample kits, certified lab safety data, and live on-site 
performance testing. 
Initial Scale Constraints: As a new entrant, AGRIARMOR faces higher 
early production costs and limited brand recognition. The business 
addresses this by focusing on high-margin local hardware channels.

Contingency Plan 
AGRIARMOR has developed an operational contingency plan to maintain 
business continuity, chemical quality, and revenue stream stability in the 
event of severe supply chain disruptions, technical failures, or market shifts. 
If severe typhoons or seasonal harvest gaps reduce tomato availability by 
over 30%, operations immediately tap into local markets. On the production 
floor, technical deviations are handled through immediate batch corrections, 
low-acid batches are fortified with bio-citric acid powder to restore the target 
PH level. Finally, to counter market resistance from skeptical industrial 
buyers who doubt bio-based chemical strength, AGRIARMOR deploys risk-
free trial kits and trial-to-buy contracts, shifting the sales focus toward labor 
savings and non-hazardous metal safety to secure steady commercial 
adoption.
