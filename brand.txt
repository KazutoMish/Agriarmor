HOW TO USE: 
1. Wipe away heavy grease, oil, or
soil coating the metal. Use a stiff
wire brush or scraper to remove
loose, flaking rust scale. 
2. Squeeze gel directly onto target
areas or tools.
3.Light Surface Flash Rust: Allow 15
to 30 minutes.  Moderate to
Severe Oxidation: Allow 1 to 3
hours.
( Reapply a thin layer if the gel begins
to dry out under direct sun)
4.Scrub treated area with a nylon
brush, brass brush, or green pad to
break up loosened rust.  
5. Rinse thoroughly with clean water
or wipe clean with a wet microfiber
cloth.
6.Immediately dry the metal surface
completely with a clean cloth or
warm air blow-dryer.  Apply a light
coat of oil, protective wax, or paint
to prevent flash rusting.

INGREDIENTS: Tomato Pulp, Kamias Pulp, Xantham Gum Booster, Mild Surfractant 
AgriArmor’s RedRevive AGRIARMOR’s RedRevive is a highly effective organic rust remover
crafted from surplus tomatoes cultivated in the Cordillera Administrative Region and
Nueva Vizcaya. RedRevive contains a bio-based active matrix made from surplus tomatoes
and kamias. It is a biodegradable, non-corrosive, and non-toxic formulation. Its thick gel
consistency ensures high surface adhesion and replaces harmful, petroleum-derived
solvent chemicals with a safe, food-grade agricultural derivative. This not only eliminates
hazardous waste disposal fees but also promotes a safer workplace environment
SAFETY, HANDLING & STORAGE GUIDELINES
 
While RedRevive is bio-based and free from
toxic 
synthetic 
mineral 
acids
(hydrochloric/phosphoric), protective gloves
and safety glasses are recommended to
prevent prolonged skin exposure or eye
irritation from natural organic acids.
Safe for use on carbon steel, cast iron, wrought
iron, and stainless steel. 
Caution: Test on a small hidden area before
applying to non-ferrous metals (aluminum,
brass, copper) or delicate painted finishes.
Store in a cool, dry place away from direct
sunlight. Keep containers tightly closed when
not in use. Product active shelf life is 24
months.
